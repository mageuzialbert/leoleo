## Publishing notes

- `WhatsApp Image 2026-10-12 at 17.19.49.jpeg` is a screenshot of another person's social story. Do not use it as a site image.
- Some images include people, contact signs, or unrelated subjects. Review each final selection before publishing.
- The photos show crops and farm activity; they do not establish claims such as organic certification, fair trade, animal welfare certification, or coffee bean origin.
- The user supplied the farm logo as `research/user-uploads/leo-leo-logo.png`; it is displayed beside the site wordmark and used as the browser icon.
- The user supplied `meat-packages.png` for Pig & Meat and `director.png` for the director's message. The meat image is framed with a tighter CSS crop so the screenshot's carousel arrows do not look like site controls.
- At the user's request, the harvest image's background mattress and stored clutter were removed with the built-in image editor. The original remains in the WhatsApp research archive. The shared project content references the edited image for both Homepage and Pili Pili. See [edit record](image-edits.md).
